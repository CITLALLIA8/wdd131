Site Name

Malva Boutique

  • “Malva” is the Spanish word for mallow, a soft purple-pink flower. The name suggests a gentle, modern and feminine style.
  • It is short, easy to remember and easy to spell, which helps with branding and recognition.
  • The logo is a letter M drawn as a clothes hanger, using the plum and rose colors of the site.

Optional domain availability: malvaboutique.com

Site Purpose

Malva Boutique is a fictional online store that offers modern and casual fashion for women. The site provides:

  • A home page that introduces the brand and highlights new arrivals.
  • A product catalog organized by clothing category (tops, dresses, pants and skirts, jackets), with images, descriptions and prices.
  • A contact page with a form where visitors can send questions to the store.

The goal is to help visitors quickly find clothing they like, understand its details, and get in touch with the store.

Scenarios

Questions a visitor from the target audience (women ages 18–40 who want comfortable, stylish clothing) might ask:

  • What new casual outfits does the store have this season?
  • How much does a dress cost, and which sizes and colors are available?
  • Can I browse only jackets or only tops?
  • How can I ask the store a question about an order or a size?

Color Scheme

The palette combines a deep plum with a soft lavender and a strong rose accent. All text and background pairs were chosen for strong contrast.

  • Plum #3B1E45

    Headings, header and footer backgrounds, navigation links.

  • Rose #B0124F

    Accents: buttons, links, prices and the active navigation item.

  • Lavender #F5F0F8

    Page background and the background of product cards.

  • Ink #2A2530

    Paragraph and body text.

  • White #FFFFFF

    Text on dark backgrounds, form fields and wireframe boxes.

Typography

Two fonts from Google Fonts will be used.

Fraunces – Headings

Modern fashion for every day

Used for the site name, page titles (h1), section headings (h2, h3) and product names. Weights: 600 and 700.

Karla – Body text

Soft, comfortable pieces that go from the office to the weekend.

Used for paragraphs, navigation, prices, buttons and form labels. Weights: 400 and 700.

Fallbacks: Georgia, serif for Fraunces and Arial, sans-serif for Karla.
